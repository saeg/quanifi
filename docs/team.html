Research Team

Quanifi is an open-source visual quantum software engineering platform developed by Neilson Ramalho as part of his PhD research at the Software Analysis and Evaluation Group (SAEG) at the University of São Paulo (USP), advised by Prof. Marcos Lordello Chaim, co-advised by Prof. Higor Amario de Souza, in research collaboration with Prof. Valter Vieira de Camargo (UFSCar).

Institutional Collaboration

The Quanifi project emerged from research collaboration between academic institutions and open-source practitioners in software engineering, programming languages, and quantum computation:

  • University of São Paulo (USP) — School of Arts, Sciences and Humanities (EACH), Polytechnic School (PCS-Poli) & Software Analysis and Evaluation Group (SAEG).

Quanifi Project

Visual Quantum Software Engineering with Apache NiFi. Composing, testing, executing and verifying quantum algorithms as enterprise dataflows.

Compatible with Qiskit, Cirq, Qrisp, pyQuil, and PennyLane across local simulators and quantum cloud hardware.
